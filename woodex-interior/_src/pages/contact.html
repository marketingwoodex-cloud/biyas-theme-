Before you write

Quick answers

The questions we hear most often at a first meeting.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes — a 60-minute studio or site visit with no obligation. You'll leave with a clear idea of scope, budget range and next steps.

What should I prepare?

A floor plan if you have one, a few reference images you like (and dislike), and an honest budget range. That's plenty for a first conversation.

How soon can you start?

Design work usually begins within two to three weeks of an agreed proposal. Workshop and site slots are booked once the design is signed off.
