Ask us anythingAsk us anything
How long does a typical project take?

A single room is usually 8–12 weeks from brief to installation. Whole apartments run 4–6 months and commercial fit-outs are programmed around your opening date.

Do you work outside Lahore?

Yes. We regularly deliver projects across Pakistan and the Gulf. Joinery is built in our workshop, test-assembled and shipped for installation by our own team.

Can I use my own contractor?

Of course. Our Design package produces a full drawing set any competent contractor can build from, and we can stay on for site visits and quality checks.

What timbers and finishes do you use?

Mostly FSC-certified European oak, American walnut and ash, alongside natural veneers. Finishes are hard-wax oils and low-VOC lacquers chosen for durability and repairability.

Is there a warranty?

All workshop-built joinery carries a 5-year warranty covering materials and workmanship, and we offer an annual maintenance visit for larger projects.
