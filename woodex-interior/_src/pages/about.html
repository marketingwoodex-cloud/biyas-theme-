About the studio

Designers and makers, under one roof

Woodex Interior is an interior design studio with its own joinery workshop. We plan, design and build spaces where natural materials, light and proportion come together with quiet confidence.

Our story

A studio built on craft, patience and honest materials

Woodex began in 2012 as a two-person joinery workshop making kitchens for friends and neighbours. As the commissions grew, so did the questions clients asked — about layouts, light, colour and how a room should feel. Today we are a studio of designers, project managers and master joiners who take a space from first sketch to final styling.

Because we design and build under one roof, nothing is lost in translation. Every panel, hinge and finish is chosen by the people who will make it.

What we believe

Principles that shape every project

01
Design for how you live

We start with routines, not references. Storage, circulation and light are planned around the way a space is actually used.

02
Honest materials

Solid timber, natural stone, lime plaster and wool — materials that age gracefully and can be repaired rather than replaced.

03
Made, not just specified

Our workshop builds what our studio draws, so details are resolved by the people who will craft them.

04
Calm, transparent process

Clear stages, fixed fees and one point of contact from concept to handover, so there are no surprises.
