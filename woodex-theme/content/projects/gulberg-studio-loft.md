## Brief

Convert a tired commercial floor into a flexible home for a designer couple.

## Approach

Instead of walls, a single oak 'furniture wall' organises the plan, hiding kitchen, laundry and wardrobe behind flush doors.

## Result

An open loft that still offers privacy, finished in twelve weeks.

## Materials & makers

The furniture wall is oak veneer on a lightweight core so it could be craned in through the window openings; the floor is a single pour of micro-cement to keep the plan reading as one room. Blackened steel frames the two glazed partitions that give the bedroom acoustic privacy without losing daylight.

## Timeline

The office strip-out and services took four weeks. The oak volume was built off-site during that period and installed in five days, with lighting commissioned in the final week.
