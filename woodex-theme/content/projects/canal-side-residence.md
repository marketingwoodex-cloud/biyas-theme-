## Brief

A young family wanted a home that felt calm despite a busy household — somewhere the noise of the city stopped at the door.

## Approach

We opened the ground floor to the courtyard and used a restrained palette of fumed oak, limestone and warm plaster. Every storage element is built in, so surfaces stay clear.

## Result

Six months from concept to move-in, delivered by our own joinery workshop and site team.

## Materials & makers

Fumed oak for every fixed element — kitchen, wardrobes, the library wall — machined and finished in our own workshop. Indus limestone underfoot on the ground floor, lime plaster on the walls so the light stays soft, and brushed brass only where a hand touches: handles, switch plates, the stair rail.

## Timeline

Six weeks of design and documentation, two rounds of renders to settle the courtyard elevation, then sixteen weeks on site. Joinery was manufactured in parallel with first-fix, so installation started the day the floors were sealed.
