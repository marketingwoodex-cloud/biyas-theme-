## Brief

Let the garments be the colour; the space should be the frame.

## Approach

Neutral stone and plaster, brass display systems that reconfigure for each season, and lighting focused on product.

## Result

A flexible store that photographs beautifully and changes with every collection.

## Materials & makers

Travertine plinths sit on a raised floor so power reaches every display point. The brass rail system was prototyped in the workshop until a single person could reconfigure a wall in under an hour. The fitting room is one curved oak volume, lit from a concealed cove.

## Timeline

Two weeks of concept and mock-ups, then a seven-week fit-out timed to the autumn collection. All joinery and the brass system were fabricated off-site and installed in the final ten days.
