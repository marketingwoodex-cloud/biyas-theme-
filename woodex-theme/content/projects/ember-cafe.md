## Brief

A specialty coffee roaster needed a flagship that felt warm at 7am and 9pm.

## Approach

We split the plan into a bright, efficient counter zone and a dimmer lounge with banquettes. Lighting scenes shift through the day.

## Result

Seating capacity increased by 30 % within the same footprint.

## Materials & makers

Hand-glazed terracotta on the counter front, a walnut bar top with an integrated brass drip rail, and banquettes upholstered in a wool that hides coffee. Pendants over the counter are on a separate circuit so the service zone can stay bright after the lounge dims.

## Timeline

Concept in two weeks, documentation in three, and a nine-week fit-out that finished a week before the launch event. The counter and all fixed seating were built in our workshop while the site was being prepared.
