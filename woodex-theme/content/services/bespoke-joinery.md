## Made in our workshop

Woodex began as a joinery workshop, and it remains the heart of the studio. Designing and building under one roof means every detail — a shadow gap, a grain match, a soft-close drawer — is resolved before it reaches your site.

## What we build

Kitchens, dressing rooms, libraries, media walls, vanities, dining tables and one-off pieces, in solid timber, veneers, lacquers and stone.

## Working with other designers

Architects and designers are welcome to commission joinery-only packages from their own drawings.

## How we price it

Every piece is quoted from a shop drawing, so the number you approve is tied to a drawing you have seen. Quotes itemise carcasses, fronts, hardware, finishes and installation separately, which makes it easy to adjust a specification without re-quoting the whole kitchen.

## Care and longevity

Joinery leaves the workshop with a care sheet for its finish — hard-wax oil, lacquer or paint — and a service visit is included in the first year to adjust doors and drawers once the house has settled.
