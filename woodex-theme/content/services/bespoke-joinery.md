## Made in our workshop

Woodex began as a joinery workshop, and it remains the heart of the studio. Designing and building under one roof means every detail — a shadow gap, a grain match, a soft-close drawer — is resolved before it reaches your site.

## What we build

Kitchens, dressing rooms, libraries, media walls, vanities, dining tables and one-off pieces, in solid timber, veneers, lacquers and stone.

## Working with other designers

Architects and designers are welcome to commission joinery-only packages from their own drawings.
