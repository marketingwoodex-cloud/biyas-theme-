## Spaces that work as hard as you do

Commercial interiors have to balance atmosphere with throughput, durability and code. We design the customer journey first, then wrap it in materials that will still look intentional after years of use.

## Sectors

Workplaces, cafés and restaurants, boutique hotels, clinics and retail — each with its own detailing, acoustic and lighting requirements.

## Delivery

We coordinate with your contractors, MEP consultants and brand team, and phase work so you can keep trading wherever possible.

## Who it's for

Founders opening a first space, operators rolling out a second or tenth branch, and developers who need a lettable shell to become a leasable experience. Our workplace clients are usually growing teams that want the office to say something about how they work, without a fit-out that stops them from working.

## How we price it

A fixed design fee per stage, then either a tendered construction contract or a turnkey price from our own team. Joinery, counters and fixed seating are built in our workshop, which is where most of the cost — and quality — control comes from. Timelines are agreed against your opening date, and we build in a two-week styling and snagging buffer before launch.
