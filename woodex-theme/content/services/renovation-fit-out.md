## One team from strip-out to styling

Renovations fail in the gaps between trades. We close those gaps by owning the programme: one schedule, one site lead, and drawings detailed enough that decisions are made on paper rather than on site.

## Scope

Full apartment and villa renovations, kitchen and bathroom remodels, office and retail fit-outs, façade and entrance upgrades.

## Transparency

Fixed-scope pricing with itemised allowances, weekly progress reports and a shared photo log.
