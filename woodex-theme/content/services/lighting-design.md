## Light is the finish you feel

The same room can feel clinical or warm depending on where the light comes from. We design in layers: quiet ambient light, focused task light, and accents that give walls, art and joinery depth.

## Deliverables

Reflected ceiling plans, circuit and switching schedules, fixture specifications, and on-site focusing once furniture is placed.

## Control

From simple dimmers to whole-home scene systems, we specify controls that your family or staff will actually use.

## Who it's for

Homeowners who feel their rooms look better in photographs than in the evening, restaurant owners who want the room to sell the food, and retailers who need products to read from the street. Architects and designers also engage us as lighting consultants on their own projects.

## How we price it

A fixed design fee based on the number of rooms or floor area, with the fitting schedule priced separately by the supplier of your choice. Focusing and commissioning visits are included, because a good scheme badly aimed is not a good scheme.
