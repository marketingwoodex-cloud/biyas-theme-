Most design studios draw; we also build. That changes the drawings.

## Timber store

Every project starts here, where boards acclimatise for a fortnight before they are touched.

## Machining and assembly

CNC handles the repetitive cuts; hands do the fitting. Each kitchen is dry-assembled in the workshop before it ever goes to site.

## Finishing

A sealed spray booth for lacquers, and a quiet corner for hand-oiling. Nothing leaves without a sample approved by the designer.
