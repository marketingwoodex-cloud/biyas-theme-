Storage is the difference between a small apartment that feels calm and one that feels crowded. The trick is to make it disappear.

1. **Full-height walls.** Floor to ceiling, wall to wall, painted the same colour as the room.
2. **The furniture wall.** One volume that contains kitchen, pantry and utility.
3. **Beds with drawers,** not boxes — easier to reach, no lifting.
4. **Door-height wardrobes** with flush doors and push latches.
5. **The hidden desk,** folded inside a cabinet for the days you work from home.
