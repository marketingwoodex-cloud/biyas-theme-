The most common question in a first meeting is also the hardest to answer in one line. Here is how the numbers break down.

## Design fee

A fixed fee for concept, design development and documentation, scaled to the size and complexity of the project.

## Build and joinery

Itemised quotations from our workshop and partner contractors, with allowances shown so that changes are transparent.

## Procurement

Furniture, lighting and accessories are bought at trade prices and passed on with a disclosed handling fee.

## Site supervision

Weekly visits, snagging and handover are included in the design fee for projects we also build.
