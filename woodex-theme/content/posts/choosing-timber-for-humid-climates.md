Timber moves. In a city where humidity swings from 20 % in winter to 80 % in the monsoon, the question is not whether your joinery will move but whether it was designed to.

## Start with moisture content

We kiln-dry every board to 8–10 % and let it acclimatise in the workshop for two weeks before machining. Skipping this step is the single most common cause of cracked doors.

## Species that behave

Teak, iroko and white oak are stable and forgiving. Sheesham is beautiful but needs careful quarter-sawing. Avoid wide solid panels in MDF-cored veneers where possible.

## Finishes

Hard-wax oils breathe and are easy to repair; catalysed lacquers seal better but show damage. We choose by room: oil for living spaces, lacquer for kitchens and baths.
