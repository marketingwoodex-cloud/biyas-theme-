Biya Jee · Punjabi Verha

The diwan

Written to be
recited

Verha — Punjabi verse of home and inner weather. It lives best out loud, which is why the house gathers monthly to read it together.

Verse of the season

The evening couplet

اَج فِیر لہور وچ مینہہ آیا، یار ملن دا ویلہ ہویا

“Today it rained in Lahore again — it became the hour for meeting friends.” A couplet first read at a monsoon mehfil; the room agreed to keep it for the season.
