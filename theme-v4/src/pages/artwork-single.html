Some works are written; this one seems lit. The leaf-thin gold sits on a dark, hand-prepared ground so that the letterform appears suspended — reading differently at morning, at dusk, by candlelight.

Made to be lived with rather than merely shown: hang it where light changes through the day and the piece will keep writing itself.
