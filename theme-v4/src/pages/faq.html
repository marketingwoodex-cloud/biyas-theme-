Everything visitors, collectors and mehfil regulars ask most — grouped the way the house groups its rooms.

I — Buying art

Q·01Are prices listed on the site?+
Most works are quoted on request while the catalogue register is verified. Write to the desk with the work's number for availability and price — every enquiry is answered personally.
Q·02Can I see a work in person before deciding?+
Please do. The house is open Tue–Sun, 11:00–19:00, and private views are arranged outside hours. Works on approval can be held for 48 hours for Lahore collectors.
Q·03Do you ship outside Pakistan?+
Yes — crated by the house crew and carried by trusted couriers. Costs and timelines are quoted with the work, and provenance papers travel with every piece.

II — Visiting

Q·04Do I need an appointment?+
Open hours welcome walk-ins. The full address is shared on confirmation of a first visit — write ahead and chai will be waiting.
Q·05Is the house family friendly?+
Very — children are given the stool by the loom and one very patient explanation. We only ask that small hands stay a step back from the gold grounds.
Q·06Where do I park?+
Street guidance is shared with the address on confirmation; a helper meets first visits at the corner. Rickshaws know the neighbourhood.

III — Commissions

Q·07How long does a commission take?+
Intimate works from a few weeks; thread folios longer — the thread decides the pace. A realistic window is agreed at the envision stage and honoured.
Q·08Can I choose the verse?+
Yes — your phrase, a family name, a line of Verha, or candidates the studio proposes. Sacred texts are approached with the respect they require; the studio will advise.

IV — Verha & events

Q·09Do I need to know Punjabi to attend a mehfil?+
No. Verse is offered in Punjabi and Urdu with English welcome; warmth needs no translation, and someone always whispers you the gist.
Q·10Can I read at the mushaira?+
Write to the house with a couplet and a reason — the floor is yours for two minutes. See Join & Collaborate.
Q·11Are evenings recorded?+
Often, and only with the room's consent. Recordings publish to the video archive as permissions clear.
