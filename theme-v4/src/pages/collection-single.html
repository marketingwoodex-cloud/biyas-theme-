Curator's note

Devotion has always been Lahore's finest draftswoman. The works in this chapter were made slowly, some across many nights — gold pigment laid over dark grounds so the lettering holds light the way a doorway holds a lamp.

The chapter began with a single Kaaba study and grew into a family of pieces concerned with radiance: calligraphy of sacred text, meditative geometry, and thread works that repeat a holy phrase until it becomes texture.

Hung together they make a room quieter. Hung apart, each carries a small piece of that quiet with it.

— with Biya Jee · note dated current season
