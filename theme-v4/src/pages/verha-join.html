How it goes

Three steps,
no gatekeeping

  1. 01

    Write two lines

    Who you are, and what brings you to the word house. Poetry optional; honesty not.

  2. 02

    Come to one mehfil

    Sit, listen, drink the chai. The house decides nothing about anyone after one evening.

  3. 03

    Find your corner

    The floor, the tech table, the door, or the calendar — every regular found a corner.
