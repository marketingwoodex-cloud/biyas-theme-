The gathering of poets

Seventeen poets,
no stage

A mushaira is democracy's oldest microphone: every poet reads, every listener rules. The house hosts the season's Long Evening — and smaller circles year-round.

The flow of the evening

How a mushaira runs

  1. 01 — Rang

    The opening colour

    A senior voice sets the evening's metre — the room tunes itself to it.

  2. 02 — Tarannum

    Recited & chanted

    Poems offered in tarannum — sung recitation — and in plain speech, by choice.

  3. 03 — Misra

    The challenge line

    A line is offered; any poet may answer it before the night ends. Many do.

  4. 04 — Daad

    The applause economy

    Daad — applause — is the only currency. The house adds chai.
