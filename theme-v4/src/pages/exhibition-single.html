Curator's note

Hang gold low and the room learns humility. For this show the house was re-lit to candle-strength: works glow from dark grounds, and visitors instinctively lower their voices — which is precisely the point.

Sacred Light gathers the devotional chapter: Kaaba studies, calligraphy of sacred text, and thread folios that repeat holy phrase into texture. Walk it slowly, clockwise, and the rooms behave like a single sentence.
