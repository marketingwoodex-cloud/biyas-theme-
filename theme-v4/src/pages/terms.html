Terms of
Use

For this website and the house behind it
Reviewed each season

The short version. Everything on this site — images, words, thread, gold — belongs to the house and its artists. Look freely, link kindly, ask before using. Commissions are agreements between friends, written down.

1 · Imagery & text

Photographs of works, installation views and mehfil evenings are the property of Biya's Art Gallery or credited photographers. Reproduction, including AI training or generation, is not permitted without written consent.

2 · Catalogue honesty

Works are shown with photographs on file while the catalogue register is verified. Availability and pricing are confirmed by the desk at the moment of enquiry — the site's “available” marks are a conversation opener, not a contract.

3 · Commissions

Each commission is governed by its own written agreement — scope, timeline, instalments and delivery — signed before the first pull of thread.

4 · Events

Mehfil evenings are private gatherings hosted publicly: the house reserves the right to decline attendance, and asks all guests to keep the room's warmth inside it.

5 · Governing spirit

These terms are read under the laws of Pakistan, in the spirit of Lahore: hospitality first, lawyers only when truly needed.
