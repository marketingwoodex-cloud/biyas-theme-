The literary practice

The line, the thread,
the image

For Biya Jee, language and visual art are not separate rooms. Both begin with attention — to feeling, rhythm, colour and the life held beneath the surface.

The poet's voice

Words are another
way to see.

Her published Punjabi poetry provides another entrance into the gallery's world — a space where words can be read slowly and images can be felt as language. Ghazals and nazams sit beside the visual work; her collections return, again and again, to the many dimensions of ishq — love as devotion, longing and memory.
