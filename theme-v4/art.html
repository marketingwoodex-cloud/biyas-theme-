The practice of Biya Jee

Her
Language

Three vocabularies, one grammar: pigment, gold and thread — all of them writing.

I — Works on paper

The patient page

Handmade studies — sacred text written at the hour it deserves, on paper that takes weeks to prepare. The Surah Rehman folios remain the discipline's spine.

Browse works on paper →

II — Gold & pigment

Letters that burn quietly

Gold leaf over umber grounds: calligraphy meant for low light and long looking. These pieces anchor the Sacred Light chapter and commission requests alike.

See Sacred Light →

III — Thread

The Third Art

The signature practice — verse translated into weave, folio by folio. Touched as often as looked at, and never twice the same in changing light.
