ویرھا — پنجابی دا کلیہ

The Word
House

Verha is Punjabi verse in its natural state: spoken, answered, argued with and applauded. This house keeps that state alive — once a month, and always out loud.

What is Verha?

Not poetry read.
Poetry shared.

Verha belongs to the oral tradition of Punjab — verse carried in memory, offered at gatherings, answered by other voices. A mushaira is not a reading; it is a conversation with intervals of applause.

The house hosts this tradition monthly: a mehfil of neighbours, poets and strangers where Punjabi and Urdu verse is recited, discussed and kept warm. No stage separates the speaker from the listener — only chai.
