Dialogues, recorded & kept

Two chairs,
one question

Muqalma is the word house's dialogue tradition — a craftsperson and a thinker, one honest question, and an audience allowed to interrupt politely.

From the last muqalma

An excerpt

Thread & Verse · recorded in the house
Transcript pending publication

The poet
“Everyone praises patience in your thread work. Isn't patience just fear of finishing?”

Biya Jee
“Finishing is easy — the thread arrives there on its own. Patience is what I spend deciding what deserves the arrival.”

The poet
“And verse? Same answer?”

Biya Jee
“Shorter. A couplet is a thread with only two pulls.”
