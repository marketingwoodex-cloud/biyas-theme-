An artist-led gallery is only as alive as the hands it gathers. The house shows its founder's work and invites selected artists to share its rooms each season.

A·01 — resident

Biya Jee

Calligraphy · The Third Art · verse

Founder of the house and its resident hand. Her practice moves between pigment, gold and woven thread — always in service of the written word, in Urdu, Punjabi and Arabic.

The open easel

Invited artists · announced per season

Each season the house keeps one easel open for an invited voice — a calligrapher, a miniaturist, a photographer of Lahore. The register is being verified; names are announced with the exhibition calendar.
