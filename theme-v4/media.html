Featured exchange

“The thread decides
the pace.”

Q — You could work faster in any other medium. Why thread?

A — Because it refuses to hurry, and I needed a teacher that refuses. A pen can be rushed; a room can be rushed. Thread cannot. Whatever pace you pull it at becomes visible forever — so it taught me the pace I wanted everywhere else in life.
