Home · Projects · Golden Leaves

P·01 — Residence · Lahore

Golden
Leaves

Seven folios ascending a staircase — gold on dark grounds, lit from below, pacing the client's evening walk from door to bedroom.

The brief

Quiet drama,
floor by floor

ScopeFolio wall · 7 works
MediumsGold pigment · thread
Delivered2026 · installed in two visits

The family asked for one thing: a wall that gets more interesting the slower you climb it.

The answer was a sequence, not a picture. Each folio answers the one below — the verse fragment growing bolder as the stairs turn — so the wall reads like a couplet split across seven lines.

Lighting was tuned to floor level: the gold warms as the evening does, and the topmost folio catches the landing lamp last.
