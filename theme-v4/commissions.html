How it goes

Four movements, one work

A conversation about the room, the light and the words.

We begin with where the work will live — photographs help, visits help more. If you don't yet have a phrase, the studio will bring candidates; if you do, we test its patience together.

Sketches, palette plates and a mock of the wall.

You receive composition studies and material plates — gold weights, ground tones, thread direction — with a scale drawing of the work on your actual wall.

The making — reported, not livestreamed.

Larger works take weeks. You receive two progress letters with plates, and one veto moment at half-way. After that, the thread decides the pace.

Hung, lit, and left to glow.

House crew installs in Lahore; crating and trusted couriers carry it worldwide. Every commission leaves with its provenance card and a maintenance note.
