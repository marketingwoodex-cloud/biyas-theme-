05 — The founder

A poet's heart.
An artist's eye.

Biya Jee is a Punjabi poet, social worker and columnist from Lahore. Her three collections of Punjabi poetry explore ishq — love as devotion, longing, memory and the force that gives art its inner life.

At the gallery, that literary voice becomes a living visual world: thoughtfully curated collections, innovative encounters and work that keeps opening with time.

· Chairperson, Punjabi Virsa· President, Arts & Culture· Published on Folk Punjab
