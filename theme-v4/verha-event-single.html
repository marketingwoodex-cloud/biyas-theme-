Monsoon
Couplet

An open evening of rain-verse. The season couplet is read aloud for the first time; the floor then belongs to whoever brought a poem.

The evening

How it unfolds

  1. 19:00

    Doors & chai

    Arrive, settle, meet the room. Shoes optional, verse mandatory-adjacent.

  2. 19:45

    The season couplet

    Biya Jee opens the evening with the new couplet, in Punjabi and Urdu.

  3. 20:15

    Open floor

    Recitals from guests and members — two minutes a voice, gently enforced by chai.

  4. 21:30

    Muqalma minute & close

    One question argued kindly, then the room exhales.
